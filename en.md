# Privacy Policy

**Sand Drop Puzzle** (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, for how long, and with whom it is shared.

---

## 1. App information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| App name | Sand Drop Puzzle |
| Package name (Android) | `com.kojaem.sanddroppuzzle` |
| Developer | KoJaem |
| Price | Free (contains ads) |
| Ads | Yes (Google AdMob: rewarded and interstitial ads) |
| Account / login | None |
| Overview | A puzzle game where falling blocks crumble into sand and you clear a color by connecting it from wall to wall |

---

## 2. Information we process

The App **does not send your personal information to any server run by the developer.** Game data stays **on your device only**. The Google advertising SDK included in the App may process the information listed below to serve ads.

### Game data (stored on your device)

- Best score per mode and coins
- Purchased and selected themes, and stage progress
- Settings (vibration, sound, language) and whether you finished the tutorial

### Information processed by the advertising SDK (Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform)

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Data | Advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, OS version, language), app information (package name, version), ad interactions (impressions, clicks), approximate location derived from IP, diagnostics |
| When | While the App requests and shows ads |
| Processed by | Google LLC |
| Purpose | Serving and measuring ads and preventing fraud. Personalized ads depend on your consent and device settings. |

See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Result sharing

The App creates an image of the result screen in the device's temporary cache **only when you tap Share**. It hands the image to **the app you choose** (for example Instagram or a messenger). The App does not upload or keep this image. After you share it, the privacy policy of the app you chose applies.

### Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Internet, network state | Requesting and showing ads |
| Vibrate | Game haptics (can be turned off in Settings) |
| Advertising ID | Ad delivery and measurement (Google advertising SDK) |
| Wake lock, foreground service | Used internally by the Google advertising SDK |

### Information we do not collect

- Account information (email, phone number, name, social login IDs)
- Contacts, photos or gallery, microphone, camera
- Precise (GPS) location
- Usage statistics from separate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- Payment information (there are no in-app purchases)

If you contact us by email, we see only the email address and message you send us.

---

## 3. Retention

- **Game data**: kept on your device and deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
- **Advertising data**: kept and deleted according to Google's privacy policy.
- **Shared result image**: overwritten the next time you share, and deleted when you uninstall the App.
- **Support emails**: deleted after your inquiry is resolved, or kept for up to 1 year if needed to resolve a dispute.

---

## 4. Sharing and international transfer

The App does not share game data with anyone. To serve ads, the data listed in Section 2 is sent to **Google LLC**. Google may process it in the United States and other countries where it has data centers. Google keeps this data according to its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

---

## 5. Your choices

- **Consent**: users in regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK) choose on the consent screen when they first open the App. You can change your choice any time in **Settings → Privacy Options** in the App.
- **Advertising ID**: you can delete or reset it in Android **Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads**. You will then see non-personalized ads.
- **Rewarded ads**: shown only when you tap the revive button.
- **Delete data**: clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
- **Requests**: email us to ask for access, deletion, or restriction of your information. For data that Google processes, use Google's own process.

---

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that it has, we will delete that information without delay.
